# AI policy in education is hardening from guidance into enforceable assessment rules

Fresh signals from schools, regulators, and ministries show AI use is now being governed through explicit constraints on assessment, student tooling, and teacher-led implementation.

## 1. Top Story
England’s higher-ed regulator says AI has made coursework assessment "close to impossible" in some areas and is pushing institutions toward more AI-free exams and in-person defenses. <u>[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/education/2026/nov/11/ai-close-to-impossible-assess-university-coursework-regulator)</u>, <u>[Yahoo News Australia](https://au.news.yahoo.com/ai-now-universal-higher-education-184042276.html)</u>.
