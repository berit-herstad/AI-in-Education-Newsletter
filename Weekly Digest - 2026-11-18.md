# AI school policy is shifting from access to evidence

This week’s clearest pattern is practical governance: systems are tightening data controls, keeping teachers central, and demanding stronger proof that AI use improves learning.

## 1. Top Story
Australian K-12 leaders are arguing that safe AI adoption starts with standardized systems, explicit data governance, and teacher-led judgment—not tool-first deployment. <u>[The Educator](https://www.theeducatoronline.com/k12/news/safe-ai-in-schools-starts-with-data-and-teachers-experts-say/289372)</u>.
