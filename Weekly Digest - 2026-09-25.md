# The day AI literacy became school infrastructure.

Abu Dhabi’s KG–12 framework shows how AI education is moving from tool access to curriculum, testing, teacher training, and safeguards. Elsewhere, schools are reaching the same conclusion: AI readiness now means governance, not gadgets.

## 1. Top Story

**Abu Dhabi turns AI literacy into a school operating model.** <u>[Emirates 24/7](https://www.emirates247.com/uae/adek-advances-ai-literacy-initiative-across-more-than-170-private-schools/5761)</u> reports that ADEK is advancing AI Literacy across more than 170 private schools, with a KG–Grade 12 framework, year-long lesson plans, teacher toolkits, and an AI Growth Test for Grades 4–12. The initiative includes strands on conceptual understanding, critical interaction, solution design, and responsible governance, plus safeguards for data protection, cognitive agency, academic integrity, human oversight, content safety, and fairness.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**AI-ready schools need governance, not just tools.** <u>[ET Education](https://education.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/industry/ai-ready-schools-principals-call-for-structured-governance-teacher-mentorship-and-student-led-innovation-at-eteducation-roundtable/134387059)</u> says Indian school leaders called for governance committees, teacher mentorship, risk classifications, and student-led innovation.

**AI search is not automatically safer for students.** <u>[EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/perplexitys-ai-search-isnt-a-safer-choice-for-kids-than-google-youth-ai-safety-institute-finds)</u> reported that Perplexity received the lowest child-risk rating from the Youth AI Safety Institute.
