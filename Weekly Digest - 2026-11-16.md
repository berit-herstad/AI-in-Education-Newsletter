# Assessment integrity has become the new front line of education AI policy

This week’s strongest signals show schools and universities moving from AI experimentation to enforceable rules on assessment validity, student access controls, and workforce-ready AI skills.

## 1. Top Story
England’s higher-ed regulator warns that AI has made coursework assessment "close to impossible" in some areas and is urging institutions to expand AI-free assessment formats. <u>[The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/education/2026/nov/11/ai-close-to-impossible-assess-university-coursework-regulator)</u>, <u>[Yahoo News Australia](https://au.news.yahoo.com/ai-now-universal-higher-education-184042276.html)</u>.
