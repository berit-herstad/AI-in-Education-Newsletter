Higher education is formalizing AI literacy as a degree pathway

This week’s strongest signal is structural: schools and universities are moving from ad hoc AI use to formal pathways for assessment, governance, and workforce-ready skills.

1Top story
US · Higher education

AI majors are scaling from pilot to mainstream

A new WBUR report shows US universities rapidly scaling undergraduate AI majors, with Bentley, MIT, Northeastern, and WPI all expanding programs as institutions compete on AI-ready graduate outcomes. WBUR.

The shift matters beyond higher-ed branding: it signals that AI literacy is becoming a core academic track tied to employability, ethics, and cross-disciplinary training rather than an optional add-on.

AI education is moving from elective exposure to formal credentialed pathways.

Australia urges schools to follow learning science, not AI hype
AERO’s latest guidance says AI can support planning and workload, but warns against outsourcing essential student thinking and calls for clearer assessment expectations. The Educator.

A D2L/WCET/Opened Culture action guide based on leaders across six continents argues institutions should extend existing policy structures rather than start governance from scratch. PR Newswire.
Vietnam classrooms are validating understanding through process evidence
Teachers report stronger assessment reliability when students submit draft stages, critique AI output, and explain reasoning orally. Vietnam News.
