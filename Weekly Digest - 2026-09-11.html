The day AI guidance moved to the adults.

New guidance, training modules, privacy agreements, and global rules all point in the same direction: systems are defining the adult controls around classroom AI.

1Top story
UNESCO · Digital Learning Week

Global AI guidance shifts toward duty of care.

UNESCO’s Digital Learning Week produced a fresh human-centred AI message for education. A ministerial statement presented in Paris on September 8 called for rules that keep human agency, equity, teacher capacity, and learner protection at the center as AI reshapes classrooms. Coverage from Devdiscourse framed the statement as a response to fast adoption without enough safeguards.

That matters because this week’s other stories are all implementation stories: Microsoft released teacher-facing AI education guides, Google expanded Gemini modules for educators, and a US teachers union reached an AI privacy deal with Microsoft.

The next phase of AI in education is less about student prompts and more about adult duty of care.
