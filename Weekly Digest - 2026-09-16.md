# The day schools asked for proof of learning

Today’s through-line is proof. After a week of privacy standards, teacher guides, and unclear policies, the newest signal is that AI rules are not enough unless schools can show students are still learning.

## Top Story

An <u>[Ohio Capital Journal](https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/09/16/ohios-school-ai-policies-need-a-proof-of-learning-standard/)</u> essay argues that Ohio’s required school AI policies now need a “proof-of-learning” standard. Ohio law required traditional public districts, community schools, and STEM schools to adopt AI policies by July 1, 2026; the harder task is making those policies meaningful in classrooms.

That framing matters because it connects several threads from the past two weeks: AI detectors are weak, student use is widespread, teacher policy clarity is low, and tutoring works best when tools force reasoning. The most mature policy question is shifting from whether AI is allowed to how schools verify thinking.

> The next standard for school AI may be less “Was AI used?” and more “Can the student show the learning?”
