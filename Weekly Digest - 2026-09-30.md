# The day lawmakers drew a human line around AI teaching.

New Jersey’s proposed Educators Before AI Act makes explicit what many school systems are circling: AI may support instruction, but human judgment, care, and accountability remain the boundary condition for classrooms.

## 1. Top Story

**New Jersey bill would bar AI from replacing public-school teachers.** <u>[News 12](https://longisland.news12.com/2026/09/24/nj-bill-would-prohibit-ai-from-replacing-teachers-in-public-school-classrooms/6VV82EvoiCsagueCO80AxL)</u> reports that Assemblywoman Heather Simmons introduced the Educators Before AI Act, requiring public school employees to be human and limiting AI’s role in public education. The bill arrives as adaptive learning models expand, drawing a clear line: AI can support instruction, but not replace the adult who provides connection, empathy, judgment, and social modeling.
