# The day AI guidance moved to the adults

This issue’s through-line is educator infrastructure. New guidance, training modules, privacy agreements, and global rules all point in the same direction: systems are no longer asking whether AI belongs in education; they are defining the adult controls around it.

## Top Story

UNESCO’s Digital Learning Week produced a fresh human-centred AI message for education. A ministerial statement presented in Paris on September 8 called for rules that keep human agency, equity, teacher capacity, and learner protection at the center as AI reshapes classrooms. Coverage from <u>[Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/3975197-unesco-calls-for-human-centred-rules-as-ai-reshapes-education)</u> framed the statement as a response to fast adoption without enough safeguards.

That matters because this week’s other stories are all implementation stories: Microsoft released teacher-facing AI education guides, Google expanded Gemini modules for educators, and a US teachers union reached an AI privacy deal with Microsoft. The governance question is shifting from tool access to institutional responsibility.

> The next phase of AI in education is less about student prompts and more about adult duty of care.

## Research & Evidence

An IBM-commissioned Morning Consult survey covered by <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/educators-feel-confident-they-can-teach-about-ai-what-do-parents-think/2026/09)</u> found 83% of educators feel confident teaching about AI, while parent trust remains a key open question.

<u>[EDUCAUSE Review](https://er.educause.edu/articles/2026/9/scaling-experiential-learning-with-ai-texas-a-m-collaborates-with-dell-technologies)</u> profiled Texas A&M’s AI-enabled experiential learning environment with Dell Technologies, showing AI being used to structure practice rather than replace instruction.

<u>[Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-ai-students-math.html)</u> kept attention on tutoring design: AI helps math learning when it withholds answers and requires reasoning.

<u>[Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02370-2)</u> continued to track the assessment redesign wave, with professors moving away from detector-first responses.

## Worth Watching

Teacher enablement is becoming the main deployment strategy: guidance, badges, privacy deals, and training now accompany product rollouts.

Human-centred AI is turning into policy language across global and national frameworks.

Student restriction and teacher empowerment are happening at the same time, creating a two-track model for school AI.
