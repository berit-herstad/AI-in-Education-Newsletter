# The week schools drew sharper AI lines

This week’s AI-in-education news split into two directions: large systems are restricting student-facing generative AI for younger learners, while ministries and universities are formalizing AI literacy, teacher training, and workforce pathways.

## Top Story

New York City and Los Angeles, the two largest US school districts, moved from “experiment” to “guardrails” by restricting student use of generative AI tools, with New York placing a one-year moratorium on student-facing AI from preschool through eighth grade and LAUSD restricting access on school devices. The significance is less that schools are banning tools outright and more that age, contracts, and assessment integrity are becoming the control points. Coverage from <u>[Axios](https://www.msn.com/en-us/education-and-learning/general/a-growing-movement-wants-ai-out-of-schools/ar-AA2bJ2oD)</u>, <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/new-york-restricts-ai-in-elementary-and-middle-schools-will-other-districts-follow/2026/09)</u>, and <u>[USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/education/2026/09/03/schools-limit-ai-teachers-mixed-reactions/91592547007/)</u> framed the moves as part of a broader backlash against classroom tech, not just chatbots.

> The center of gravity is shifting from “Should schools use AI?” to “Who gets access, at what age, and under whose supervision?”

## Research & Evidence

New University of Toronto Mississauga research reported via <u>[Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-ai-students-math.html)</u> suggests AI tutors can help with math only when designed to hold back answers and force student reasoning.

The American Psychological Association warned, via <u>[Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-technology-engagement.html)</u>, that engagement metrics in edtech are not evidence of learning, especially when generative AI enables shallow completion.

<u>[Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02370-2)</u> highlighted how university academics worldwide are redesigning assessment, from AI-resistant exams to process-based proof of learning.

<u>[Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/240432/Nearly+98+of+Surveyed+Plovdiv+University+Students+Use+AI+as+Bulgarian+Universities+Grapple+With+Academic+Integrity)</u> reported that nearly 98% of surveyed Plovdiv University students use AI, underscoring how normalized AI has become in higher education.

## Around the Globe

🇧🇷 Brazil’s National Education Council approved AI-use guidelines for schools and universities, including risk categories and limits on automated assessment, pending ministry homologation, according to <u>[Porvir](https://porvir.org/cne-diretrizes-inteligencia-artificial-escola-universidade/)</u>.

🇲🇽 Mexico’s SEP and UNESCO signed a letter of intent to support ethical AI, digital-device, and social-media policy in education, reports <u>[La Jornada](https://www.jornada.com.mx/noticia/2026/09/07/sociedad/sep-y-unesco-acuerdan-impulsar-uso-etico-de-inteligencia-artificial-en-educacion)</u>.

🇦🇪 The UAE is rolling out an AI curriculum across public and private schools, with large-scale teacher training, per <u>[Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-to-roll-out-ai-curriculum-across-all-schools-22000-teachers-to-be-trained)</u>.

🇯🇵 Japan’s education ministry draft would expand information education and embed AI across subjects, not just computing, according to <u>[The Mainichi](https://mainichi.jp/english/articles/20260901/p2a/00m/0na/024000c)</u>.

🇮🇳 Google is running nationwide AI training for Indian educators in multiple languages throughout September, reports <u>[Education Times](https://www.educationtimes.com/article/newsroom/99742729/teachers-day-2026-google-rolls-out-nationwide-ai-training-sessions-for-indian-educators)</u>.

🇸🇬 Singapore’s ITE and Singapore Polytechnic signed AI-ready built-environment skills MOUs with Nemetschek, covering digital twins and AI-assisted compliance, per <u>[TechNode Global](https://technode.global/2026/09/03/nemetschek-ite-singapore-polytechnic-ai-built-environment-skills/)</u>.

🇦🇺 Australian school leaders face an “AI strategy gap,” with only a fraction confident they have clear plans, reports <u>[The Educator](https://www.theeducatoronline.com/k12/news/the-ai-strategy-gap-in-australian-schools/289153)</u>.

## Worth Watching

Age-gating is hardening: the US district moves, Norway comparisons, and UAE/Japan curriculum shifts all treat younger learners differently from older students.

Assessment is moving from detection to design: universities are redesigning tasks around process, oral defense, and demonstrated reasoning.

Teacher enablement is becoming infrastructure: India, UAE, and platform providers are pairing AI rollout with educator training rather than assuming tools will self-adopt.
