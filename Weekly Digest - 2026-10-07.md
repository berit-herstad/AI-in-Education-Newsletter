# Educators are trying to shape AI before AI shapes school

The week’s strongest signal is a shift from reaction to design: universities, districts, and vendors are building test beds, procurement rules, and teacher-first tools before classroom AI hardens into defaults.

## 1. Top Story

The University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education launched the Living Laboratory for Human Agency and AI, a network connecting researchers, educators, school systems, and industry partners to test AI in real educational settings. The initiative focuses on human agency, trust, safety, practice-driven innovation, AI literacy, and infrastructure for scale, according to <u>[Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/educators-are-racing-to-shape-ai-before-it-shapes-education)</u>.

That framing captures the new phase of AI in education. The question is no longer whether schools will encounter AI, but whether educators can generate evidence, norms, and design patterns before general-purpose tools define classroom practice for them.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**Living labs replace abstract AI predictions.** Penn GSE’s hub will test AI tutoring tools, educator resources, literacy strategies, and governance approaches in authentic school partnerships. <u>[Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/educators-are-racing-to-shape-ai-before-it-shapes-education)</u>.

**Procurement is becoming an evidence problem.** Districts are trying to judge whether AI products are safe, explainable, privacy-preserving, bias-aware, and actually improving learning before they buy. <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/schools-spend-billions-on-ai-but-struggle-to-figure-out-whats-worth-buying/2026/08)</u>.

**Teacher demand is specific: AI built for education.** Sanoma’s European Teacher Survey found 75% to 93% of teachers want AI tools designed for education rather than adapted from general-purpose applications. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇫🇮 **Finland.** Helsinki-based Sanoma Learning launched Sanna, an AI teacher assistant trialing in Finland, Sweden, and other European markets, built on local learning materials and national curricula. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Norway’s rapid school AI uptake remains the Nordic benchmark for urgency, with reported use in nearly three-fourths of primary/lower-secondary schools and over 90% of upper-secondary schools. <u>[VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/wreqMo/boom-i-ai-bruk-i-norsk-skole-ministeren-urolig)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Bodo’s role in the European AI Academy project keeps Norway in the curriculum-building lane, testing how schools can teach AI without turning it into a shortcut. <u>[NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/bodo-blir-eneste-kommune-i-internasjonal-prosjekt-om-kunstig-intelligens-i-skolen-1.17844687)</u>.

## 4. Around the Globe

🇺🇸 **US.** New York City is keeping student-facing generative AI out through eighth grade while requiring high-school AI literacy modules and allowing selected supervised pilots. <u>[International Business Times Singapore](https://www.ibtimes.sg/new-york-city-bans-student-facing-genai-grades-2-8-2026-27-94409)</u>.

🇰🇪 **Kenya.** UNESCO and Codemao’s youth coding initiative has trained more than 1,200 Kenyan students and established school coding clubs in 20 schools. <u>[Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261004/5e2675c0dab14d639f8325b9fffda668/c.html)</u>.

🇻🇳 **Vietnam.** Vietnam’s national AI education framework will give every grade 12 core periods per year, with schools choosing modules, subject integration, or elective activities. <u>[Vietnamnet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/ai-education-to-reach-all-vietnamese-schools-from-2026-2027-2547587.html)</u>.

🇮🇳 **India.** The Millennium AI Creators Championship named student winners across responsible AI storytelling, song, and short film challenges. <u>[LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/business-news-the-millennium-group-of-schools-announces-winners-of-millennium-ai-creators-championship-2026-celebrating-young-indias-responsible-ai-creators-7631339.html)</u>.

🇦🇪 **UAE.** Alef Education is showcasing responsible, AI-powered learning at Ai Everything Abu Dhabi 2026. <u>[Emirates News Agency on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-ae/news/other/alef-education-to-showcase-responsible-ai-powered-learning-at-ai-everything-abu-dhabi-2026/ar-AA2dl8gg)</u>.

## 5. Tools & Platforms

Teacher control is becoming the product promise: Sanna anchors AI in local curricula, procurement checklists demand explainability and privacy, and living labs turn product testing into research.

## 6. Worth Watching

Watch school-based AI test beds grow, teacher-first AI become a market category, and policy move from bans to age bands. The main trend: educator agency is becoming the design principle.
