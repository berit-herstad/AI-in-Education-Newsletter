# AI learning is becoming a systems question

The week ends with a broader pattern: AI education is no longer just about classroom use. It now stretches across open universities, procurement, teacher workload, student safeguards, and lifelong learning.

## 1. Top Story

Universitas Terbuka’s graduate school used FUSION 2026 to connect artificial intelligence with sustainable innovation, public policy, lifelong learning, and human capital development. The forum brought researchers, students, and industry practitioners together around digital transformation and cross-sector collaboration, according to <u>[Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/special-updates/uts-fusion-2026-zeroes-in-lifelong-learning-and-ai)</u>.

That framing is important because it moves AI education beyond school-age learners. The emerging question is how education systems support people as AI reshapes work, citizenship, research, and public services over a lifetime.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**District AI spending needs better filters.** US districts are being asked to judge privacy, explainability, bias, student-data ownership, and learning value while vendors move faster than state and federal guidance. <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/schools-spend-billions-on-ai-but-struggle-to-figure-out-whats-worth-buying/2026/08)</u>.

**Teacher demand points to purpose-built AI.** Sanoma’s European Teacher Survey found 75% to 93% of teachers want AI tools designed for education, not adapted from general-purpose applications. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

**Living labs turn adoption into inquiry.** Penn GSE’s Living Laboratory for Human Agency and AI is designed to test AI tools, governance, pedagogy, and literacy strategies in real education settings. <u>[Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/educators-are-racing-to-shape-ai-before-it-shapes-education)</u>.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇫🇮 **Finland.** Sanoma Learning’s Sanna assistant, developed by a Helsinki-based company, is built on local learning materials and national curricula, with trials in Finland, Sweden, and other European markets. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Norway’s debate remains shaped by rapid AI uptake and concern over when children should meet AI, with earlier reporting showing high use across both primary/lower-secondary and upper-secondary schools. <u>[VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/wreqMo/boom-i-ai-bruk-i-norsk-skole-ministeren-urolig)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Bodo’s role in the European AI Academy keeps Norway in the curriculum-building lane, testing responsible use, teacher networks, and age-appropriate classroom practice. <u>[NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/bodo-blir-eneste-kommune-i-internasjonal-prosjekt-om-kunstig-intelligens-i-skolen-1.17844687)</u>.

## 4. Around the Globe

🇮🇩 **Indonesia.** Universitas Terbuka’s FUSION 2026 framed AI alongside lifelong learning and human capital. <u>[Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/special-updates/uts-fusion-2026-zeroes-in-lifelong-learning-and-ai)</u>.

🇰🇪 **Kenya.** UNESCO and Codemao’s youth coding initiative has trained more than 1,200 Kenyan students and established coding clubs in 20 schools. <u>[Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261004/5e2675c0dab14d639f8325b9fffda668/c.html)</u>.

🇻🇳 **Vietnam.** Vietnam’s national AI education framework sets 12 core periods per grade each year. <u>[Vietnamnet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/ai-education-to-reach-all-vietnamese-schools-from-2026-2027-2547587.html)</u>.

🇺🇸 **US.** New York City is keeping student-facing GenAI out through eighth grade while requiring high-school AI literacy modules and selected supervised pilots. <u>[International Business Times Singapore](https://www.ibtimes.sg/new-york-city-bans-student-facing-genai-grades-2-8-2026-27-94409)</u>.

🇮🇳 **India.** The Millennium AI Creators Championship named student winners in creative challenges tied to sustainable cities and responsible AI. <u>[LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/business-news-the-millennium-group-of-schools-announces-winners-of-millennium-ai-creators-championship-2026-celebrating-young-indias-responsible-ai-creators-7631339.html)</u>.

## 5. Tools & Platforms

Tools are being judged by institutional fit: teacher assistants move into curriculum territory, procurement checklists become learning safeguards, and living labs make adoption testable.

## 6. Worth Watching

Watch adult learning become a frontline AI issue, procurement become pedagogy, and age-banded policy gain momentum. The main trend: AI education is becoming lifelong-learning infrastructure.
