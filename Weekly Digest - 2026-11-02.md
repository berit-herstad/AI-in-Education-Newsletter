# AI in education is moving from adoption to evidence demands

The past week showed a clear shift: schools and universities are no longer asking whether to use AI, but how to prove it improves learning, protects systems, and preserves trust.

## 1. Top Story
U.S. policy discussions now emphasize that AI deployment in schools is outrunning the evidence base, while district cyber reporting shows implementation pressure and resource constraints happening at the same time. <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/we-need-research-on-ai-in-schools-the-feds-should-fund-it-report-says/2026/10)</u> and <u>[StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/more-than-half-of-k-12-schools-reported-cyber-incidents-amid-staffing-funding-gaps/)</u>.
