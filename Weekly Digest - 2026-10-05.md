# The new AI risk is dependence, not just cheating

Fresh research and this week's policy signals point to a sharper agenda for schools: protect student thinking, design AI around learning, and make assessment evidence harder to outsource.

## 1. Top Story

A new study of 1,029 Chinese high school and university students argues that generative AI dependence is not one behavior but two pathways: deep cognitive absorption and a more utilitarian habit of using AI because it reduces effort. The research, summarized by <u>[BIOENGINEER](https://bioengineer.org/why-students-fall-into-the-grip-of-generative-ai-new-study-maps-the-psychological-pathways/)</u>, applies the I-PACE behavioral-addiction model to student AI use.

That matters for schools because the remedies differ. A student mesmerized by chatbot interaction may need attention-management routines; a student using AI as an academic shortcut may need tasks that rebuild the value of independent effort.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**Psychological literacy joins AI literacy.** The dependence study links overreliance to cognitive absorption and perceived usefulness, suggesting AI guidance should teach learners how their own attention and motivation respond to tools. <u>[BIOENGINEER](https://bioengineer.org/why-students-fall-into-the-grip-of-generative-ai-new-study-maps-the-psychological-pathways/)</u>.

**Educators feel ready, but training is uneven.** An IBM/Morning Consult survey reported by Education Week found 83% of educators confident teaching about AI, while many still learn through social media, friends, or family. <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/educators-feel-confident-they-can-teach-about-ai-what-do-parents-think/2026/09)</u>.

**Learning science warns against cognitive offloading.** Microsoft's education principles emphasize AI that supports learning rather than replacing student thinking, with age-appropriate controls and scaffolded practice. <u>[Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/16/microsofts-commitment-for-ai-in-education/)</u>.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Norwegian debate continues to focus on teacher control and age-appropriate use after reports that AI tools are already used in nearly three-fourths of primary/lower-secondary schools and over 90% of upper-secondary schools. <u>[VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/wreqMo/boom-i-ai-bruk-i-norsk-skole-ministeren-urolig)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Bodo remains Norway's only municipality in the European AI Academy project, testing curriculum, tools, and teacher networks for responsible classroom AI. <u>[NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/bodo-blir-eneste-kommune-i-internasjonal-prosjekt-om-kunstig-intelligens-i-skolen-1.17844687)</u>.

🇫🇮 **Finland.** Generation AI remains a Nordic reference point after UNESCO recognized it for curriculum-scale AI-in-education work rather than one-off tool adoption. <u>[Gulf Digital News](https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1405930)</u>.

## 4. Around the Globe

🇰🇪 **Kenya.** KNEC warned teachers not to use AI to complete learners' School-Based Assessment tasks, clarifying that the concern is assessed evidence, not a blanket ban. <u>[Education News Kenya](https://educationnews.co.ke/knec-warns-teachers-against-using-ai-to-complete-learners-school-based-assessment-tasks/)</u>.

🇻🇳 **Vietnam.** VNU and Google launched AI Arena: Viet Nam 2026, a national university competition where teams build proof-of-concept prototypes with Gemini and AI Studio. <u>[Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/Sci-Tech/1801103/viet-nam-launches-national-generative-ai-competition-for-university-students.html)</u>.

🇮🇳 **India.** Kakatiya University announced an AI-enabled curriculum and industry-linked reforms for students in North Telangana. <u>[Hindu Dayashankar](https://hindudayashankar.com/education/kakatiya-university-to-transform-education-through-ai-enabled-curriculum/)</u>.

🇦🇪 **UAE.** Alef Education is positioning responsible, AI-powered learning as a showcase theme at Ai Everything Abu Dhabi 2026. <u>[Emirates News Agency on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-ae/news/other/alef-education-to-showcase-responsible-ai-powered-learning-at-ai-everything-abu-dhabi-2026/ar-AA2dl8gg)</u>.

🇺🇸 **US.** EDUCAUSE 2026 closed with AI reportedly topping higher education's IT priority list for the first time. <u>[Tech Times on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/ai-tops-higher-ed-priority-list-for-first-time-as-educause-2026-closes/ar-AA2drClU)</u>.

## 5. Tools & Platforms

Microsoft is foregrounding privacy, safety, age controls, and educator oversight; Google is backing structured student prototyping through AI Arena; and Alef is pairing personalization with responsible-learning language. The platform story is shifting from access to governed practice.

## 6. Worth Watching

Watch assessment rules get more specific, AI literacy absorb well-being literacy, and higher education move from experiments to budgets. The main trend: student thinking is becoming the protected asset.
