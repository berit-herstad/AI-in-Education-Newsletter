# The week guardrails became the product.

Florida turned school AI rules into parent notices, opt-ins, tool lists, and companion bans, while India and South Korea showed what supervised AI practice can look like in classrooms.

## 1. Top Story

**Florida made the guardrails concrete.** <u>[WUSF](https://www.wusf.org/education/2026-09-17/florida-approves-statewide-ai-rules-for-k-12-schools-and-colleges)</u> reports that the state approved rules requiring districts and charter schools to write AI policies into internet-safety plans, notify parents when classroom tools are approved, provide opt-in/out choices, maintain approved-tool lists, and keep AI from replacing teachers’ judgment. The sharpest line: schools may not use AI tools designed to simulate friendship, companionship, or emotional relationships with students.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**EngageAI was renewed through 2031.** <u>[UNC-Chapel Hill](https://www.unc.edu/posts/2026/09/14/ai-research-to-improve-k-12-stem-learning-gets-funding-boost/)</u> says NSF added another $20 million for adaptive K-12 STEM lessons, long-term learner memory, teacher authoring tools, and classroom studies.

**Source-aware AI is becoming assessable.** <u>[Donga Science](https://www.dongascience.com/en/news/79939)</u> described a Korean inquiry competition where teachers reviewed students’ questions, sources, conversation histories, and evolving hypotheses.

**Homework evidence keeps weakening.** <u>[Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/ai-croatian-students-homework-teachers-education/)</u> reports that Croatian schools and universities are questioning whether take-home essays still prove independent learning.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇳🇴 **Norway** remains a reference point for age-gating: <u>[Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/09/03/before-new-york-city-norway-had-already-banned-ai-in-schools)</u> noted its primary-school generative-AI ban.

🇳🇴 **Oslo** keeps privacy in focus after banning camera-equipped AI smart glasses in schools. <u>[The Local Norway](https://www.thelocal.no/20260910/oslo-bans-smart-glasses-in-schools)</u>

🇩🇰 **Denmark** remains the proof-of-learning model, pairing written work with oral defenses and tighter exam controls. <u>[Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/07/denmark-tightens-rules-on-secondary-school-students-to-prevent-ai-cheating)</u>

## 4. Around the Globe

🇮🇳 **India:** Sikkim rolled out AI-FunZone in 71 government schools for adaptive reading and math practice. <u>[Jagran Josh](https://www.jagranjosh.com/news/sikkim-launches-ai-powered-learning-programme-in-71-government-schools-187585)</u>

🇻🇳 **Vietnam:** Hanoi will teach AI content to every Grade 1-12 student, with 12 lessons and no separate grades. <u>[Tuoi Tre](https://news.tuoitre.vn/hanoi-starts-to-teach-ai-to-all-students-from-grade-1-103260914143808402.htm)</u>

🇰🇷 **South Korea:** Jinsan Middle School used source-verified AI to track student reasoning. <u>[Donga Science](https://www.dongascience.com/en/news/79939)</u>

🇦🇪 **UAE:** The national K-12 AI curriculum remains a leading systemwide rollout. <u>[Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/education-industry/uae-to-introduce-ai-curriculum-across-public-private-schools-whats-to-know/)</u>

🇳🇵 **Nepal:** Curriculum reform continues to pair AI readiness with indigenous knowledge. <u>[Kathmandu Post on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-xl/asia/nepal/nepal-begins-overhaul-of-school-curriculum-with-ai-and-indigenous-knowledge-in-focus/ar-AA2cc96P)</u>

## 5. Tools & Platforms

AI-FunZone shows adaptive practice becoming school infrastructure; Science Donga Air Edu shows source trails becoming a product feature; and <u>[Training Journal](https://www.trainingjournal.com/2026/content-type/opinion/ai-in-learning-2026-a-day-built-for-sensemaking-not-just-adoption/)</u> argues workplace learning needs sensemaking, not tool churn.

## 6. Worth Watching

Permission systems are becoming part of pedagogy. Process records are becoming the new anti-cheating layer. And companion AI may be the next hard boundary for school policy.
