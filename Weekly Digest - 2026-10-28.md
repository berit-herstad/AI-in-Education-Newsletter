# Schools are writing AI rules after deployment, not before

This week’s education signals show AI moving faster than governance. Institutions are now redesigning assessment, cyber controls, and teacher workflows while tools are already in daily use.

## 1. Top Story
A new U.S. K-12 cybersecurity report shows most districts faced incidents in the past year, while many still operate with limited staffing and budgets and are formalizing AI safeguards in parallel. AI policy is becoming operational risk policy. <u>[StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/more-than-half-of-k-12-schools-reported-cyber-incidents-amid-staffing-funding-gaps/)</u>.
