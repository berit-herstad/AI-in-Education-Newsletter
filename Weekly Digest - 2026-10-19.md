# AI education is being rewritten around professional judgment

The week’s strongest signal is that AI learning is moving from generic literacy to role-specific judgment: doctors, teachers, students, and school leaders now need different AI rules and skills.

## 1. Top Story

A national survey of internal medicine residency directors found that formal AI education remains inconsistent and underdeveloped, even as residents and clinicians already use AI tools informally. The study, published in the Journal of General Internal Medicine and summarized by <u>[BIOENGINEER](https://bioengineer.org/us-medical-residencies-are-flying-blind-on-ai-training-national-survey-finds/)</u>, points to gaps in faculty expertise, validated curricula, and time for training.

The issue is bigger than medicine. It shows the next stage of AI education: not just giving people access, but teaching them when to trust, verify, reject, or contextualize AI output inside professional practice.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**Medical AI education lacks a shared curriculum.** Residency programmes are aware of the need for AI instruction but constrained by limited faculty expertise and rapidly changing tools. <u>[BIOENGINEER](https://bioengineer.org/us-medical-residencies-are-flying-blind-on-ai-training-national-survey-finds/)</u>.

**AI-era medical education is being redesigned.** OpenEvidence and Johns Hopkins Medicine are collaborating on clinical training that protects reasoning, evidence use, pathophysiology, and patient-centered judgment. <u>[Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openevidence-johns-hopkins-medicine-partner-on-ai-education-and-care/)</u>.

**Student assessment must look beyond final answers.** Vietnamese educators argue that AI makes polished assignments less reliable as evidence, pushing assessment toward process, reasoning, source checking, and oral explanation. <u>[Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/society/1802270/ai-forces-teachers-to-rethink-teaching-and-assessment.html)</u>.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇫🇮 **Finland.** Sanoma Learning’s Sanna assistant keeps the Nordic signal on curriculum-grounded, teacher-reviewed AI rather than generic chatbot adoption. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Norway’s AI-in-school debate remains shaped by high reported use and concern over when younger pupils should encounter AI. <u>[VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/wreqMo/boom-i-ai-bruk-i-norsk-skole-ministeren-urolig)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Bodo’s participation in the European AI Academy keeps Norway connected to curriculum-building, teacher networks, and responsible classroom use. <u>[NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/bodo-blir-eneste-kommune-i-internasjonal-prosjekt-om-kunstig-intelligens-i-skolen-1.17844687)</u>.

## 4. Around the Globe

🇺🇸 **US.** Internal medicine residencies are inconsistent on formal AI education, despite clinicians already facing hallucination, privacy, bias, and verification risks. <u>[BIOENGINEER](https://bioengineer.org/us-medical-residencies-are-flying-blind-on-ai-training-national-survey-finds/)</u>.

🇵🇭 **Philippines.** Google, DepEd, CHED, and TESDA launched a free AI Educator Series for Filipino teachers, with modules on classroom use, administration, research, privacy, and academic integrity. <u>[CoinGeek](https://coingeek.com/google-partners-with-deped-ched-tesda-to-launch-ai-educator-series-for-filipino-teachers/)</u>.

🇻🇳 **Vietnam.** Educators are calling for assessments that require students to explain how they use AI, verify information, and show reasoning rather than submit only polished outputs. <u>[Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/society/1802270/ai-forces-teachers-to-rethink-teaching-and-assessment.html)</u>.

🇦🇪 **UAE.** Alef Education says its AI-powered learning tools drew interest across the Gulf, Africa, and Asia, with auto-grading highlighted as a teacher workload tool. <u>[Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/alef-education-attracts-international-interest-in-ai-powered-learning-solutions-at-ai-everything-abu-dhabi-2026-1588202)</u>.

🇮🇳 **India.** NCERT and Google for Education are training educators on AI-supported communication and digital workflows. <u>[Jagran Josh](https://www.jagranjosh.com/news/ncert-to-launch-4-day-online-training-for-teachers-on-effective-communication-from-october-12-188240)</u>.

## 5. Tools & Platforms

Tools are being judged by the expertise they preserve: clinical AI must teach verification, teacher tools emphasize workload and oversight, and assessment tools face the reasoning test.

## 6. Worth Watching

Watch professional schools become AI literacy laboratories, teacher training scale through national platforms, and assessment shift from product to process. The main trend: AI literacy is becoming professional judgment.
