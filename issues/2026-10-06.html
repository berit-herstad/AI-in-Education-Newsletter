AI is moving upstream of the classroom

This week's strongest signal is that education AI now shapes decisions before, around, and beyond lessons: how students choose schools, how districts buy tools, and how systems define readiness.

1Top story
Canada · Enrollment

Business schools discover they are already being ranked by AI

Gander's new Canadian Business School Index tracked how AI systems answer common MBA applicant questions across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The report found that top schools appear in more than 93% of AI answers, while Reddit is cited more often than any individual school website, according to Yahoo Finance.

The finding turns AI in education into an enrollment story as much as a classroom story. If prospective students are using AI as a first advisor, institutions need to know not only what they teach with AI, but what AI is telling learners about them.

AI is becoming the new front door to education: a recommender, reputation layer, and admissions influence before a student ever reaches a campus website.
