The day AI literacy became school infrastructure.

Abu Dhabi’s KG–12 framework shows how AI education is moving from tool access to curriculum, testing, teacher training, and safeguards. Elsewhere, schools are reaching the same conclusion: AI readiness now means governance, not gadgets.

1Top story
UAE · Systemwide AI literacy

Abu Dhabi turns AI literacy into a full school operating model.

Emirates 24/7 reports that Abu Dhabi’s education authority is advancing AI Literacy across more than 170 private schools, with a KG–Grade 12 framework, year-long lesson plans, teacher toolkits, and an AI Growth Test for Grades 4–12.

The important detail is system design. The initiative includes strands for conceptual understanding, critical interaction, solution design, and responsible governance, plus safeguards for data protection, cognitive agency, academic integrity, human oversight, content safety, and fairness. It targets 19,000 educators for professional development, making AI literacy an institutional capability rather than a one-off classroom activity.

AI literacy is becoming less like a lesson topic and more like a school operating system.
2Research & evidence

Evidence is shifting toward maturity models and safer defaults.

AI-ready schools need governance, not just tools.
School leaders in India argued that AI readiness requires leadership vision, curriculum alignment, teacher capacity, assessment reform, and documented governance rather than simply allowing ChatGPT in classrooms. ET Education.
AI search still needs a child-safety layer.
A Youth AI Safety Institute assessment found Perplexity was not a safer choice for children than Google, citing weak age-appropriate behavior, homework answer-giving, and uneven source quality. EdTech Innovation Hub.
Teacher AI literacy can be embedded in existing professional learning.
Edutopia recommends small practices — showing prompts, citing AI-generated media, modeling tool use, and disclosing AI-assisted handouts — to build staff confidence without waiting for a dedicated AI workshop. Edutopia.

6Worth watching

Three patterns to track.

AI literacy is becoming measurable.
Abu Dhabi’s AI Growth Test points toward systems assessing AI capability over time, not merely declaring access.
Governance is moving into school org charts.
AI leads, governance committees, risk classifications, and incident protocols are becoming part of school operations.
Student-safe defaults may become procurement criteria.
Age-aware search, homework modes, data rules, and crisis responses are likely to matter when schools choose platforms.
