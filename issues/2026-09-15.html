The day unclear AI policy became the story.

After a week of safety standards, teacher guides, curriculum rollouts, and student restrictions, the biggest signal is that schools still do not have policies teachers can actually use.

1Top story
Stanford AI Index · Policy clarity

Only 6% of teachers say their school’s AI policy is clear.

The Next Web reported a sharp finding from Stanford’s AI Index education coverage: only 6% of teachers say their school’s AI policy is clear, while four in five US students are using AI for schoolwork and about half of schools have no policy. The story also notes that adoption is accelerating outside the West.

That makes “policy clarity” the missing layer between ambition and practice. Teachers need usable answers: what is allowed, what must be disclosed, what counts as learning evidence, and what data protections are guaranteed.

The problem is no longer whether AI has entered school. It is whether adults can explain the rules.
