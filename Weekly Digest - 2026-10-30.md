# Education leaders are shifting from AI pilots to AI guardrails

This week, schools and universities moved from experimentation toward policy enforcement: tighter assessment rules, stronger cyber controls, and explicit governance for AI use.

## 1. Top Story
A major U.S. K-12 cybersecurity survey found incidents are widespread while AI use grows faster than formal safeguards in many districts. AI policy is now directly tied to staffing, incident response, and continuity of operations. <u>[StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/more-than-half-of-k-12-schools-reported-cyber-incidents-amid-staffing-funding-gaps/)</u>.
