# AI policy is becoming more specific than AI hype in education

Across this week’s signals, education systems are moving from broad AI adoption language to concrete rules, governance architecture, and evidence demands tied to classroom and institutional risk.

## 1. Top Story
Large U.S. districts are shifting from principles to enforceable AI operating rules, while cyber incident data shows implementation pressure rising faster than safeguards. <u>[FFXnow](https://www.ffxnow.com/2026/11/02/new-fcps-policies-governing-educational-technology-use-of-ai-start-to-take-shape/)</u> and <u>[StateScoop](https://statescoop.com/more-than-half-of-k-12-schools-reported-cyber-incidents-amid-staffing-funding-gaps/)</u>.
