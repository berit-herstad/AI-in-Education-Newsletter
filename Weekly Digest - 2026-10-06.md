# AI is moving upstream of the classroom

This week's strongest signal is that education AI now shapes decisions before, around, and beyond lessons: how students choose schools, how districts buy tools, and how systems define readiness.

## 1. Top Story

Gander's new Canadian Business School Index tracked how AI systems answer common MBA applicant questions across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The report found that top schools appear in more than 93% of AI answers, while Reddit is cited more often than any individual school website, according to <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/gander-ai-releases-canadian-business-161500566.html)</u>.

The finding turns AI in education into an enrollment story as much as a classroom story. If prospective students are using AI as a first advisor, institutions need to know not only what they teach with AI, but what AI is telling learners about them.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**Districts are struggling to know what is worth buying.** School systems are spending into a fast-moving AI market while carrying much of the burden for vetting privacy, security, learning value, and bias. <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/schools-spend-billions-on-ai-but-struggle-to-figure-out-whats-worth-buying/2026/08)</u>.

**Adoption is ahead of infrastructure.** A 2026 data roundup reports widespread student and teacher AI use, weak detection, uneven teacher training, and a growing mismatch between adoption and institutional readiness. <u>[Memeburn](https://memeburn.com/ai-in-education-statistics/)</u>.

**Learning science keeps returning to cognitive effort.** Microsoft's education principles warn against cognitive offloading and argue for tools that scaffold practice rather than replace student thinking. <u>[Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/16/microsofts-commitment-for-ai-in-education/)</u>.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Norway's earlier surge in school AI use remains the Nordic benchmark for urgency, with reported use in nearly three-fourths of primary/lower-secondary schools and over 90% of upper-secondary schools. <u>[VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/wreqMo/boom-i-ai-bruk-i-norsk-skole-ministeren-urolig)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Bodo's role in the European AI Academy project keeps Norway in the curriculum-building lane, testing how schools can teach AI without turning it into a shortcut. <u>[NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/bodo-blir-eneste-kommune-i-internasjonal-prosjekt-om-kunstig-intelligens-i-skolen-1.17844687)</u>.

🇫🇮 **Finland.** Generation AI remains a Nordic counterpoint: a university-led, curriculum-scale model recognized by UNESCO rather than a tool-first rollout. <u>[Gulf Digital News](https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1405930)</u>.

## 5. Tools & Platforms

AI search is becoming an enrollment tool, procurement now needs learning evidence, and privacy and safety are turning into product requirements. The platform question is shifting from access to governance.

## 6. Worth Watching

Watch enrollment teams monitor AI visibility, procurement become curriculum work, and responsible-AI claims face demands for proof. The main trend: education AI is becoming infrastructure.
