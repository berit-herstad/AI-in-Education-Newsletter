# AI education is widening from school years to lifelong learning

This week’s education-AI signal is broader than classrooms: universities, open universities, publishers, and school systems are treating AI as a lifelong-learning infrastructure question.

## 1. Top Story

Universitas Terbuka’s graduate school used its 2026 FUSION conference to connect artificial intelligence with sustainable innovation, public policy, human capital, and lifelong learning. The forum brought researchers, industry practitioners, and students together around cross-sector questions, according to <u>[Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/special-updates/uts-fusion-2026-zeroes-in-lifelong-learning-and-ai)</u>.

That is a useful shift: AI is no longer just an edtech layer for students already inside formal schooling. It is becoming part of how systems think about reskilling, open education, adult learners, and the human-capital pipeline.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**Districts need better filters before buying AI.** US districts are weighing privacy, explainability, bias, student data ownership, and learning value while vendors move faster than policy guidance. <u>[Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/schools-spend-billions-on-ai-but-struggle-to-figure-out-whats-worth-buying/2026/08)</u>.

**Teacher demand is for education-specific AI.** Sanoma’s European Teacher Survey found 75% to 93% of teachers want AI tools designed for education rather than adapted from general-purpose systems. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

**Living labs are becoming the research model.** Penn GSE’s Living Laboratory for Human Agency and AI is built to test tools, governance, pedagogy, and AI literacy in real educational settings. <u>[Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/educators-are-racing-to-shape-ai-before-it-shapes-education)</u>.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇫🇮 **Finland.** Helsinki-based Sanoma Learning launched Sanna, an AI teacher assistant built on local learning materials and national curricula, with trials in Finland, Sweden, and other European markets. <u>[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/sanoma-learning-launches-sanna-ai-090000076.html)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Norway’s school AI debate remains shaped by rapid adoption and concern over age-appropriate use, with earlier reporting showing high use across both primary/lower-secondary and upper-secondary schools. <u>[VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/wreqMo/boom-i-ai-bruk-i-norsk-skole-ministeren-urolig)</u>.

🇳🇴 **Norway.** Bodo’s participation in the European AI Academy keeps Norway connected to curriculum-building, teacher networks, and responsible classroom use. <u>[NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nordland/bodo-blir-eneste-kommune-i-internasjonal-prosjekt-om-kunstig-intelligens-i-skolen-1.17844687)</u>.

## 4. Around the Globe

🇮🇩 **Indonesia.** Universitas Terbuka’s FUSION 2026 linked AI with lifelong learning, sustainable development, and human capital. <u>[Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/special-updates/uts-fusion-2026-zeroes-in-lifelong-learning-and-ai)</u>.

🇰🇪 **Kenya.** UNESCO and Codemao’s youth coding initiative has trained more than 1,200 Kenyan students and established coding clubs in 20 schools. <u>[Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261004/5e2675c0dab14d639f8325b9fffda668/c.html)</u>.

🇻🇳 **Vietnam.** Vietnam’s national AI education framework gives every grade 12 core periods per year, with schools choosing modules, subject integration, or elective activities. <u>[Vietnamnet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/ai-education-to-reach-all-vietnamese-schools-from-2026-2027-2547587.html)</u>.

🇺🇸 **US.** New York City is keeping student-facing GenAI out through eighth grade while requiring high-school AI literacy modules and allowing selected supervised pilots. <u>[International Business Times Singapore](https://www.ibtimes.sg/new-york-city-bans-student-facing-genai-grades-2-8-2026-27-94409)</u>.

🇮🇳 **India.** The Millennium AI Creators Championship named winners in creative challenges around sustainable cities and responsible AI. <u>[LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/business-news-the-millennium-group-of-schools-announces-winners-of-millennium-ai-creators-championship-2026-celebrating-young-indias-responsible-ai-creators-7631339.html)</u>.

## 5. Tools & Platforms

Platforms are being judged by fit and control: Sanna makes curriculum alignment a feature, procurement checklists become learning safeguards, and living labs turn tool adoption into inquiry.

## 6. Worth Watching

Watch adult learning move into the AI education center, purpose-built AI compete with general chatbots, and governance become an implementation skill. The main trend: AI literacy is becoming lifelong-learning infrastructure.
