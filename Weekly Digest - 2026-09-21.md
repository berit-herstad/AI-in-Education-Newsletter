# The week AI pilots met policy guardrails.

Sikkim moved AI into daily literacy and numeracy practice while Florida wrote binding rules for student-facing tools. The pattern is widening: schools want adaptive support, but only with teachers, evidence, and limits still in the loop.

## 1. Top Story

**Sikkim put AI into the school day, not just the strategy deck.** <u>[Jagran Josh](https://www.jagranjosh.com/news/sikkim-launches-ai-powered-learning-programme-in-71-government-schools-187585)</u> and <u>[ET Education](https://education.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/school-education/sikkim-rolls-out-ai-powered-learning-programme-in-71-government-schools/134379337)</u> report that Sikkim has launched AI-FunZone across 71 government schools in Gangtok district, targeting Classes 3-5 with daily adaptive practice in English, Nepali, and mathematics. The system listens to reading, detects math errors, adjusts practice, and gives teachers dashboards; the state says teachers remain central, while infrastructure gaps are still acknowledged.

## 2. Research & Evidence

**EngageAI gets another five years.** <u>[UNC-Chapel Hill](https://www.unc.edu/posts/2026/09/14/ai-research-to-improve-k-12-stem-learning-gets-funding-boost/)</u> says NSF renewed the EngageAI Institute through 2031 with another $20 million for adaptive K-12 STEM lessons, year-long student memory, teacher tools, and classroom studies.

**Citation-based AI showed engagement gains.** <u>[Donga Science](https://www.dongascience.com/en/news/79410)</u> reported that high-school science students using source-backed AI saw engagement rise from 2.82 to 3.34 on a five-point scale.

**Process records matter.** <u>[Jinsan Middle School](https://www.dongascience.com/en/news/79939)</u> used source-verified AI in an inquiry competition where teachers could inspect how students’ questions evolved, not just the final report.

## 3. Nordic Focus

🇳🇴 **Norway** remains the European reference point for age-gating: <u>[Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/09/03/before-new-york-city-norway-had-already-banned-ai-in-schools)</u> noted its primary-school generative-AI ban preceded New York’s move.

🇳🇴 **Oslo** is also drawing a privacy line around wearables; <u>[The Local Norway](https://www.thelocal.no/20260910/oslo-bans-smart-glasses-in-schools)</u> reported that AI smart glasses were barred from schools.

🇩🇰 **Denmark** remains the proof-of-learning model: <u>[Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/07/denmark-tightens-rules-on-secondary-school-students-to-prevent-ai-cheating)</u> says written work now faces oral defense and tighter exam controls.

## 4. Around the Globe

🇺🇸 **US:** Florida approved statewide AI rules for K-12 and colleges, requiring parent notice/choice, approved-tool lists, data protections, and banning companion-style AI relationships. <u>[WUSF](https://www.wusf.org/education/2026-09-17/florida-approves-statewide-ai-rules-for-k-12-schools-and-colleges)</u>

🇻🇳 **Vietnam:** Hanoi will teach AI content to every student from Grade 1 to 12, with 12 lessons and no separate grades. <u>[Tuoi Tre](https://news.tuoitre.vn/hanoi-starts-to-teach-ai-to-all-students-from-grade-1-103260914143808402.htm)</u>

🇭🇷 **Croatia:** Teachers say AI-written homework is forcing schools to rethink whether take-home assignments still prove learning. <u>[Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/ai-croatian-students-homework-teachers-education/)</u>

🇰🇷 **South Korea:** Jinsan Middle School used sourced AI to support student-designed science inquiries. <u>[Donga Science](https://www.dongascience.com/en/news/79939)</u>

🇦🇪 **UAE:** The AI curriculum rollout remains the clearest systemwide K-12 curriculum case in the region. <u>[Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/education-industry/uae-to-introduce-ai-curriculum-across-public-private-schools-whats-to-know/)</u>

🇳🇵 **Nepal:** The Grade 1-12 curriculum overhaul is still unfolding, with AI and indigenous knowledge named as priorities. <u>[Kathmandu Post on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-xl/asia/nepal/nepal-begins-overhaul-of-school-curriculum-with-ai-and-indigenous-knowledge-in-focus/ar-AA2cc96P)</u>

## 5. Tools & Platforms

AI-FunZone is adaptive remedial infrastructure; Science Donga Air Edu is pushing source-backed AI inquiry; and <u>[Training Journal](https://www.trainingjournal.com/2026/content-type/opinion/ai-in-learning-2026-a-day-built-for-sensemaking-not-just-adoption/)</u> framed workplace AI learning as sensemaking for L&D and HR, not just tool adoption.

## 6. Worth Watching

Adaptive pilots now need infrastructure proof; companion AI is becoming a red line; and assessment is shifting toward process evidence — show the reasoning, not just the finished answer.
